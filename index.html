Media Studio

A desktop app (Windows) for creating short narrated videos and sharing them to TikTok.

What it does

  • Writes a video script from a topic using an AI assistant.
  • Generates scene images (Canva, Google Flow) and voice-over (text-to-speech) on your computer.
  • Assembles the final MP4 locally with FFmpeg.
  • Optionally uploads the finished video to your own TikTok account, either as a draft in your TikTok inbox or as a post, only after you sign in with TikTok and confirm each upload.

TikTok integration

Media Studio uses TikTok Login Kit to let you connect your account and the Content Posting API to upload videos you created. You choose the caption, privacy level and interaction settings before anything is posted. You can disconnect at any time from the app's Settings or from TikTok's "Manage app permissions".
