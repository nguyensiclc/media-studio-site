Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Summary

Media Studio runs entirely on your computer. It has no server of its own and does not collect, sell or share your personal data with the app's authors.

Data stored on your computer

  • Your projects, scripts, images, audio and rendered videos (in your user folder).
  • App settings, including API credentials you enter.
  • If you connect TikTok: the access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok and your TikTok open ID, stored locally so you stay signed in. Deleting them ("Disconnect" in Settings) removes them.

Data sent to TikTok

Only when you choose to: the video file you confirm, its caption, and the privacy / interaction settings you select. The app reads your TikTok display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic) to show which account will be used. TikTok processes this data under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Other services

Features you enable may send content to third-party services you configure (for example image generation or AI text generation). Those requests go directly from your computer to those services under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can disconnect TikTok at any time in the app, revoke access in TikTok's "Manage app permissions", and delete your local data by deleting the app's folders.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
